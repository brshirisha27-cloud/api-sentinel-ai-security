Composite Risk Scoring Architecture & Formula

API Sentinel utilizes a composite risk calculation engine that combines supervised classification probabilities, unsupervised multivariate outlier distance, and deterministic security heuristics.

// Mathematical Risk Formula:
Risk Score = Clamp0To100( (0.45 × RF_Risk) + (0.25 × IF_Anomaly) + (0.30 × Heuristic_Penalty) )
Where:
• RF_Risk = (P(Suspicious) × 50) + (P(Attack) × 92)
• IF_Anomaly = Max(0, Min(100, (0.25 - DecisionFunction) × 160))
• Heuristic_Penalty = (PayloadRisk × 6.5) + (FailedLogins × 7.0) + RatePenalty
